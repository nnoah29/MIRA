## MIRA 2.4.0

Voice dictation, a long-term memory for the AI assistant, and a much lighter app when it is idle or in the background.

### What's new
- **Voice dictation.** A microphone button in the AI chat and in the composer types what you say, live, with punctuation. Recognition runs entirely on your computer: your voice and the dictated text never leave it. French and English are supported, each with its own model (about 57 MB, downloaded once after you confirm, removable from Settings → Voice). The microphone opens as soon as you click and nothing you say while the model loads is lost; animated bars follow your voice so you can see it is being heard. After the first download, dictation starts by itself.
- **Assistant memory.** The assistant can remember things across conversations (`remember`, `recall_memory`, `forget_memory`), recalls relevant memories in the chat on its own, and you can review and delete them in the new **Memory** tab of Settings. Embeddings are computed locally or by the provider you choose.
- **Writing style.** MIRA analyses your own writing locally and gives the assistant a style instruction, so drafts sound like you. A new "what you should know about me" field lets you add context yourself.
- **Mail cards in the chat.** When the assistant cites messages, they appear as cards you can open.
- **Local AI model on Windows.** The local model option of the assistant now also works on Windows.

### Lighter and faster
- **Idle CPU.** A blinking cursor in the mail list kept redrawing the window every frame even when it was hidden. On our test machine a hidden MIRA window went from about 60 % of a CPU core to about 0.1 %.
- **Memory.** Memory freed by the app is now returned to the system. After a normal session the resident memory dropped from about 440 MB to about 280 MB on Linux, and hibernation releases a little more.
- **Dictation start-up.** The speech model now loads about twice as fast (about 1.7 s instead of 3.4 s on our test machine).

### Settings
- New **Memory** and **Voice** tabs; dictation no longer sits in Messaging.

### Known limits
- **Voice dictation is not fully functional on Windows and Android yet.** Linux is the only platform tested with a real microphone; on Windows and Android the feature has not been tested and may not work. Names, spelled-out acronyms and English words inside French sentences are the weak spots. Linux needs `parecord` (PulseAudio or PipeWire) installed.
- The speech models come from a third party under a community licence; MIRA does not redistribute them, the app downloads them from their original source.
- The memory and writing-style features are new and may change.
- The Linux fixes for idle CPU and memory were measured on Linux only.
