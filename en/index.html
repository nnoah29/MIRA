Just a mail client, but prettier

MIRA reads and sends your mail, on Linux and on Android. I made it because the mail clients I found on Linux didn't appeal to me: too austere, paid, or stuck in a browser.

Download MIRA Source code

Linux · Android · Free · MIT licence

What's inside

Look

A bit of character

Big headlines, square corners, one bright colour. Dark or light theme, in English or French.

Colours

Yours, not mine

Pick the accent colour, or let MIRA take the colours of your wallpaper.

Accounts

All in one place

Gmail, Outlook, Yahoo or any IMAP server, with a unified inbox if you want one.

Offline

Works without a network

Your mail is copied to your device. Read and reply on the train: everything goes out when the network returns.

Day to day

Small extras

Full-text search, snooze a message, undo send, schedule a message, keyboard shortcuts.

Privacy

Nothing goes through me

No MIRA server. Remote images and trackers stay blocked until you allow them.

Download

Debian, Ubuntu, Mint, Pop!_OS

mira-mail_1.0.1_amd64.deb Download

Then, in a terminal

sudo apt install ./mira-mail_1.0.1_amd64.deb

Fedora, RHEL, Rocky, Alma, openSUSE

mira-mail-1.0.1-1.x86_64.rpm Download

Then, in a terminal

sudo dnf install ./mira-mail-1.0.1-1.x86_64.rpm

Any Linux distribution

MIRA-1.0.1-x86_64.AppImage Download

Then, in a terminal

chmod +x MIRA-1.0.1-x86_64.AppImage && ./MIRA-1.0.1-x86_64.AppImage

Arch, Manjaro, EndeavourOS

mira-mail-1.0.1-x86_64.tar.gz Download

Then, in a terminal

tar xzf mira-mail-1.0.1-x86_64.tar.gz

Phones running Android 7 and later

mira-mail-1.0.1-android-arm64-v8a.apk Download

Install and update · Linux, no sudo

curl -fsSL https://github.com/nnoah29/MIRA/releases/latest/download/install.sh | bash

Run the same command again to get the latest version.

Linux: glibc 2.34 or newer (Ubuntu 22.04, Debian 12, Fedora 35 and later) and GTK 3. To store passwords, a keyring such as GNOME Keyring or KWallet. Checksums are published with each release.
